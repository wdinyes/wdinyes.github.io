Watched *Backrooms* last weekend, finally.  The way it just reached down into my lower lizard brain and flicked the terror button in the same way the dream episode of *MASH* did really did a number on me.

But what was more . . . horrifying?  Is that the horrific things were exactly the same AI slop renders of real things that sort of bug you.  [I'm sure that was the point](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/backrooms-kane-parsons-ai-defeats-the-purpose-filmmaking-1236766375/).  Stop signs written backwards.  Chairs with odd proportions or weird numbers of legs.  People with too many fingers or eyes or noses.

And right on the heels of that, my team at work sent me an AI render of my voice doing a voice over for a video.  Now, because I trust my team, I know they're not going to use it for evil.  But . . . wow.

The last time they did this, it was pretty clearly **not me**.  The cadence was wrong.  The way I tone down at the end of sentences was off.  This new version, well, my wife could tell it wasn't me, there were some really subtle things.  My stepdaughter was more surprised that it wasn't actually me.  I've sent the file to my other kids, we'll see what they have to say, since they have the most experience with my voice.

It's an odd experience.
